# ORIN-SWEEP-1 DECISIONS (each entry recorded BEFORE the runs it affects)

## D1 — 2026-08-20T14:20Z — budget knob is `num_pts`, as pre-registered
The prereg designates "the tracker's max-features parameter" as the single budget knob; in the frozen config that is `num_pts` = 200 (both families). `docs/icra27/EXECUTION_PLAN.md` §E6 (written before the prereg) argued that a `num_pts`-only sweep would be confounded by the updater's `max_msckf_in_update` cap (50 KAIST / 40 EuRoC). The prereg governs and is frozen; we note the objection rather than act on it: (i) the sweep region of interest is downward (50–150), where the tracker supply is at or below the cap, so the cap binds least exactly where compliance is decided; (ii) tracking is the dominant per-callback cost on this board (C5: tracking ≈12 ms of a 19 ms warm-up callback), so `num_pts` is the knob that actually moves the wall-clock; (iii) the realized accepted-landmark population is NOT printed at INFO verbosity and verbosity is not changed (it would alter timing), so the population is not logged — this is recorded as a descriptive limitation in the report, not patched. No other parameter is touched.

## D2 — 2026-08-20T14:20Z — throttle definition (INVALID_THERMAL)
Sampled at 1 Hz on the host for every cell: all thermal-zone temperatures and `cooling_device*/cur_state`. A cell is INVALID_THERMAL iff, within [cell start, cell end + 13 s], any non-fan cooling device (`cpufreq-cpu0`, `cpufreq-cpu4`, `*-throttle-alert`, `hot-surface-alert`, `devfreq-gpu`) reports `cur_state > 0`, or `cpu-thermal` ≥ 70.0 °C (the board's lower passive trip point). DVFS frequency variation under `schedutil` is NOT a throttle (it is the frozen clock policy). On INVALID_THERMAL the driver cools 300 s and enqueues exactly one repeat (suffix `t`); both cells are kept and reported.

## D3 — 2026-08-20T14:20Z — thermal start-state gate and cool-down (not tuning)
To keep cells thermally comparable without the C5 fixed 120 s sleep (which would push 194 runs past the night), each cell starts only when `cpu-thermal` ≤ 50 °C (wait ≤ 600 s, wait time recorded), and the inter-cell cool-down ends at ≤ 46 °C or 90 s. C5 peaks were ≤ 48 °C, so in practice this is a short pause. Clocks, power mode, fan policy are never touched.

## D4 — 2026-08-20T14:20Z — where the two deltas live
The estimator source and `config/` are read-only on both machines (path scope `ov_msckf ov_core ov_init ov_eval config`). The budget and the elimination flag enter as ROS private parameters through two sweep launch files kept OUTSIDE the frozen tree (`/data/tooling/sweep/` on the board; `scripts/icra27/orin_sweep/` on the desktop), each generated from the frozen C5 launch by adding exactly two `<arg>`s and one `<param>` (diffs in `STEP0_TABLE.md`). The cell runner is the C5 runner byte-for-byte.

## D5 — 2026-08-20T14:20Z — ordering within phases
Prereg/prompt phase order is kept (a: KAIST {F,100}; b: {75,150} then {50,300}; c: EuRoC grid; d: top-ups). Inside each phase the queue is repeat-major (r1 of every cell, then r2, then r3) and alternates S1/N0 at each (sequence, budget) so any slow drift touches both modes equally and so r1 of every cell exists before any r2 — the accuracy trajectory (deterministic, 1 per cell) is secured first.

## D6 — 2026-08-20T14:20Z — power capture fix and the frozen energy convention
C5's `tegrastats` processes outlived their cells (sudo swallows SIGINT); the driver now `pkill -KILL`s tegrastats 13 s after each cell ends, so logs do not overlap. The C5 energy convention is kept verbatim and was reproduced on the C5 raw logs to the decimal before use (161.8 J / 51.8 mJ, 149.6 J / 47.9 mJ, 149.3 J / 80.9 mJ, 128.2 J / 69.4 mJ): idle mean = mean VDD_IN over the first 60 samples of the C5 60 s idle capture = **3038 mW**; energy above idle = Σ over VDD_IN samples with timestamp in [capture start, capture start + `/usr/bin/time` wall-clock + 12 s] of (VDD_IN − idle mean) × 1 s (declared 1 Hz period; actual tick ≈1.04 s, as in C5); energy per update = energy above idle ÷ rows of `timing_openvins.csv`. A fresh 60 s idle capture is taken tonight and reported alongside as drift information; the 3038 mW C5 value stays the baseline in every energy number.

## D7 — 2026-08-20T14:20Z — accuracy and coverage on Orin cells
ATE/RPE use the frozen CDSC-1R4 core unchanged (`cross_dataset_pair_evaluator` → `kaist_pair_evaluator`: bounded quaternion projection, evo 1.31.1 nearest association ≤ 0.01 s, SE(3) Umeyama without scale, translation ATE RMSE, 1 m all-pairs RPE) through a thin adapter that converts `state_estimate.txt` with the repo's `scripts/cp0/openvins_to_tum.py` and reads the cell layout the Orin runner produces (no run_manifest). Ground truth: `ov_data/kaist_vio/{rotation_fast,circle,square_head}.txt`, `ov_data/euroc_mav/MH_05_difficult.txt` (hashes in the aggregate). Coverage is welded to every ATE: coverage % = (state span − Σ state gaps > 0.2 s) ÷ camera-callback span of the cell (first→last `timing_openvins.csv` timestamp); a cell with `roslaunch_exit ≠ 0` or no state rows reports status FAILED and no ATE. Cross-architecture comparisons to desktop numbers stay descriptive and are never merged into desktop tables.

## D8 — 2026-08-20T14:20Z — statistics
Medians and IQR over repeats for p50/p95/p99/max, compliance %, energy/update, peak RSS; the single deterministic trajectory per cell for ATE/RPE (byte identity across repeats is reported as a count with denominator — it is a free determinism check, not a gate). B*(mode, family) per prereg := largest grid budget whose median compliance across repeats ≥ 95 % on EVERY sequence of the family is NOT the prereg wording; the prereg says "median deadline compliance across repeats ≥ 95 %" per (mode, dataset family). We evaluate it as: for each budget, the median over all repeats of all sequences in the family; and ALSO report the per-sequence B* so the family-level number can be read against its components. CL-2/CL-3 are evaluated per sequence at F exactly as worded (median-over-repeats ratio ≤ 0.85 / ≤ 0.80 on ≥ 2 sequences).
